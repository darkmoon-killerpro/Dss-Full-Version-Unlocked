## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Dual Playback Decks** — DSS features two powerful playback decks supporting various digital audio formats, including MP3, M4A, MP4, WAV, OGG, and CDA, to mix your favorite songs effortlessly.
- **CUE Points** — With 8 CUE points, users can set precise markers within tracks, allowing for creative transitions and flawless mixing.
- **Scratch Control** — Depending on your mixing style, control playback with scratch or search modes for perfect track manipulation.
- **Native DSP Effects** — Enhance your mixes with built-in digital signal processing effects, providing dynamic audio enhancements.
- **VST & DirectX Support** — Extend your audio capabilities by integrating third-party plugins for customized effects and enhancements.

### Advanced Capabilities
- **MIDI Controller Compatibility** — Connect any standard MIDI device to gain hands-on control over DSS, allowing for a more immersive DJing experience.
- **Oversampling Options** — Improve playback quality with oversampling, enhancing the sound of each track for a professional touch.
- **ASIO & WDM Driver Support** — Ensure compatibility with various audio interfaces, allowing seamless integration with your sound card for optimal performance.
- **Beat Grid** — Achieve perfect song synchronization with the beat grid feature, making it easy to mix tracks in time with each other.
